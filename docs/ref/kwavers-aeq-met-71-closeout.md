## KWAVERS-AEQ-MET-71 — Type instantaneous pressure vs pressure amplitude across all bubble-dynamics algorithms [arch] — done

priority: architecture; needs: none; basis: 6671f6d6339d257ecc4d0e3a79b12e1e48994539 (branch `triplet-pulse-imaging` at the Nov 18, 2026 contract fix); scope: every bubble-dynamics drive-pressure boundary in `crates/kwavers-physics/src/acoustics/bubble_dynamics/` and `crates/kwavers-physics/src/analytical/cavitation/`, `crates/kwavers-simulation/src/imaging/triplet_pulse.rs`, `crates/kwavers-therapy/src/therapy/lithotripsy/cavitation_cloud.rs`, `crates/kwavers-therapy/src/therapy/microbubble_dynamics/service/`, `crates/kwavers-therapy/src/therapy/theranostic_guidance/nonlinear3d/cavitation/forward.rs`, `crates/kwavers-solver/src/forward/bubble_dynamics/plugin/`, `crates/kwavers-python/src/bubble_bindings/` and `crates/kwavers-python/src/analytical_bindings/cavitation/`; milestone: M-imaging-capability

- **Outcome:** passing a pressure amplitude where an instantaneous pressure belongs (or vice versa) is a compile error, not a runtime surprise. Two distinct newtypes wrap the Aequitas quantity in `crates/kwavers-physics/src/acoustics/bubble_dynamics/acoustic_pressure.rs`: `InstantaneousPressure(Pressure)` — the instantaneous far-field pressure `p_∞(t) − p0` every bubble model takes — and `PressureAmplitude(Pressure)` — a harmonic-drive amplitude, convertible to a drive only by sampling the tone (`sample` / `instantaneous_at` / `instantaneous_at_phase` / `rate_at_phase`). `dp_dt` is typed with Aequitas `PressureRate` directly (different dimension, no newtype needed). A plain Aequitas `Pressure` cannot make the distinction: instantaneous pressure and amplitude share the dimension M L⁻¹ T⁻² and Aequitas 0.3.1 has no kind/marker mechanism for same-dimension quantities (the dimension is the only `PhantomData`); the newtype design follows the precedent ADR 070 set when it rejected a same-dimension quantity on semantic-role grounds. Conversion to raw `f64` is confined to formula boundaries (`as_pascals()` / `*as_base()` at the equation or waveform definition); dense arrays stay scalar numerical storage (ADR 070). `BubbleParameters.driving_amplitude` is typed as `PressureAmplitude` (record-only; no model reads it). The Nov 18, 2026 contract fix (Keller & Miksis 1980, JASA 68(2):628–633, Eq. 3.4 — models take a general instantaneous `p_∞(t)`, the amplitude form is a CW specialisation) and the KW-PHYS-002 `p_sat(T_w)` fix are preserved exactly; no test value changed.
- **Evidence:** before this work the contract lived in prose only, and the triplet-pulse port had already shipped the exact failure mode it guards against: `KellerMiksisModel::calculate_acceleration` treated `p_acoustic` as an amplitude and multiplied by `sin(ωt)` itself while the port passed an already-formed instantaneous pressure, so the bubble was driven at 2f0 with no f0 at all (diagnosed Nov 17, 2026; fixed Nov 18, 2026 in 22 files).
