## KW-PHYS-003 — Latent-heat term is unphysical: L_v goes negative above ~1056 °C, and a phantom latent flux is applied when mass transfer is off [patch] — done (fixed 2026-11-19)

priority: correctness; needs: none; scope: `latent_temperature_rate` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/bubble_dynamics/keller_miksis/thermodynamics/temperature.rs`) and `latent_heat_water_j_per_kg` in `thermodynamics/phase.rs`; milestone: none

- **Outcome:** the latent-heat coupling in the Keller–Miksis temperature update is physical at all temperatures the solver reaches: L_v ≥ 0 everywhere (L_v → 0 at the critical temperature), the flux is zero above T_c, and no latent heat is applied when mass transfer is disabled — so thermal-on 3P/1P ratios and peak temperatures are not contaminated by a wrong-signed latent term.
- **Driver:** gap review of the triplet-pulse simulation (Nov 19, 2026; `~/workspace/kwavers-work/review_2026-11-19.md` §E). Two defects in the same function, both verified by reading the code:
  - **(A) L_v evaluated at the bulk gas temperature with a linear fit that goes negative.** `latent_heat_water_j_per_kg(T_C) = 2.501e6 − 2369·T_C` J/kg is a linear Watson/NIST fit valid ~0–100 °C (its own tests cover only 0–100 °C). `latent_temperature_rate` calls it with `t_bubble`, the bulk gas temperature, which reaches ~3200 K during collapse (KW-PHYS-002 four-way data: both-on T_max ≈ 3200 K). The fit crosses zero at 1055.7 °C (1328.9 K) and is −4.43 MJ/kg at 3200 K. Negative latent heat is unphysical: by Watson's rule L_v → 0 at the critical point (647 K) and is undefined above it. Since the latent term is `−L_v·ṁ/(n·c_v)`, a negative L_v flips its sign during every violent collapse — condensation cools the gas and evaporation heats it. The port's thermal-on 3P/1P ratios (0.376 / 0.358 / 0.142 for R0 = 2 / 3 / 5 µm) were computed with this bug present; the R0 = 5 µm value (0.142 vs thermal-off 0.219, −35%) is a candidate consequence, unquantified until the ratios are re-measured post-fix. The four-way Rmax split is insensitive (4.0× both-on), so the radius is not the affected quantity; the temperature — and anything downstream of it (e.g. sonoluminescence) — is.
  - **(B) Phantom latent flux when mass transfer is off.** `update_temperature` is gated only on `use_thermal_effects`; `update_mass_transfer` is gated separately on `use_mass_transfer` (`keller_miksis/mod.rs`, both documented as independent no-ops). In the thermal-on / mass-off configuration the temperature ODE applies a Hertz–Knudsen latent-heat flux (computed from p_sat(T_liquid) − p_vapor, the same formula as the mass update) that never changes n_vapor — latent energy without mass transfer, violating the mass–energy coupling of Kreider et al. 2011 Eq. 20. The two fluxes did NOT in fact agree exactly when both are on: the latent term evaluated p_sat with the Antoine correlation (`p_sat_water_pa`) while the mass update uses the Wagner equation (`thermo_calc.vapor_pressure`), which differ by ~0.5% at 20 °C, and the mass update's equilibrium clamp (which the latent term ignored) trims the applied step whenever it overshoots saturation — measured Nov 19, 2026 to bind in production runs (see the dn/dt addendum below). Only the mixed configuration was inconsistent in kind; the both-on case was self-consistent only to ~0.5% plus the clamp trims. Magnitude (estimated): during collapse the phantom term is order 10¹¹ K/s, comparable to the adiabatic term. Impact measured so far: small on Rmax (four-way split 4.0× vs 4.2×) but the T_max split (mass-off ≈700 K vs both-on ≈3200 K, from the KW-PHYS-002 diagnosis) is consistent with a large temperature effect — attribution not isolated experimentally.
- **Acceptance:** L_v ≥ 0 for all T, L_v = 0 above T_c, and the Hertz–Knudsen flux is zero above T_c; the latent term is identically zero when `use_mass_transfer` is false (asserted by a test, e.g. the latent component of dT/dt == 0 in that configuration); the four-way split (3 µm, 800 kPa, 1 MHz, 8 cycles) still gives Rmax/R0 ≲ 20 in all four combinations; the port thermal-on 3P/1P ratios are re-measured and recorded with a documented reason for any move toward/away from the thermal-off values; the existing thermodynamics tests still pass.
- **Literature basis:** Kreider, Crum, Bailey & Sapozhnikov 2011, J. Acoust. Soc. Am. 130:3511–3530, §2B, Eq. (15) (flux set to zero above the critical temperature) and Eq. (20) (interface energy balance coupling latent heat to the actual mass flux); Storey & Szeri 2000, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 456:1685–1709, §2(d) (Hertz–Knudsen "loses validity during the terminal stages of the bubble collapse"; P_sat at the interface temperature); Watson's rule (L_v → 0 at T_c); Prosperetti, A. 1991, "The thermal behaviour of oscillating gas bubbles", J. Fluid Mech. 222:587–616, DOI 10.1017/S0022112091001234 (asymptotic thermal analysis, near-equilibrium only — it exists; the "does not exist" note in the KW-PHYS-002 entry and in `port_step3_diagnosis.md` is wrong and should be corrected alongside this fix); Prosperetti 1977, JASA 61:17–27 (foundational linear-thermal paper). The p_sat side of the same coupling was already fixed to the wall temperature by KW-PHYS-002; this entry is the L_v side of the same interface-temperature rule.
- **Proposed fix (applied Nov 19, 2026 — see Resolution, including the dn/dt coupling addendum):** evaluate L_v at the interface/liquid temperature (as p_sat already is), clamp L_v ≥ 0 with L_v → 0 at T_c, and zero the flux above T_c per Kreider; gate the latent term on `use_mass_transfer`, and compute it from the actual dn/dt applied by `update_mass_transfer`, so the temperature and mass equations can never disagree about the flux.

- **Resolution (fixed Nov 19, 2026):** both defects fixed in `kwavers-physics`, both trees, no push, no git commit.
  - (A) `latent_heat_water_j_per_kg` (`thermodynamics/phase.rs`) now returns 0 at and above the critical temperature (T_c = 647.096 K = 373.946 °C, Watson's rule) and clamps the linear fit at ≥ 0 below it; `latent_temperature_rate` (`thermodynamics/temperature.rs`) evaluates L_v at the interface/liquid temperature T_w (293.15 K), never at the bulk gas temperature, and zeroes the Hertz–Knudsen flux when the interface temperature exceeds T_c (Kreider et al. 2011, Eq. 15). The T_c switch-off is dormant while T_w is held at room temperature and activates if a varying wall temperature (Yasui 1996) is ever introduced; the bulk gas temperature is deliberately NOT used for the test (that is the KW-PHYS-002 error pattern).
  - (B) The latent term is gated on `use_mass_transfer` (Kreider Eq. 20). The gate was on the flag rather than on the applied dn/dt because the operator split runs the temperature update before the mass update in the same sub-step (`adaptive_integration/integrator.rs`), so no applied dn/dt exists yet when the latent term runs; the flag is the exact condition under which `update_mass_transfer` is a no-op. That limitation was removed the same day by the dn/dt coupling below — no split reordering was needed.
  - New tests (4): `latent_heat_never_negative`, `latent_heat_zero_above_critical_temperature` (`phase.rs`); `latent_term_zero_when_mass_transfer_off`, `latent_term_cools_on_evaporation_at_collapse_temperature` (`thermodynamics/tests.rs`, via a `pub(crate)` `latent_temperature_rate` re-exported test-only). Existing thermodynamics tests still pass; full `cargo test -p kwavers-physics --lib`: 1587 passed, 0 failed (1583 before + 4 new).
  - Four-way split re-measured (3 µm, 800 kPa, 1 MHz, 8 cycles): off/off 4.2×, on/off 4.1×, off/on 4.2×, on/on 4.0× (was 4.2 / 4.0 / 4.2 / 4.0). All ≲ 20. The on/off 4.0 → 4.1 move is within the integrator's stiff-collapse truncation noise (the test notes maxima are captured before truncation); the both-on value is unchanged at 4.0×.
  - Port thermal-on 3P/1P ratios re-measured (0.26 MPa, flat-top, cycles 6–10): at 1.5f0, **0.257 / 0.299 / 0.143** for R0 = 2 / 3 / 5 µm (was 0.376 / 0.358 / 0.142 with the bug present); at f0, 0.136 / 0.034 / 0.001 (was 0.129 / 0.015 / 0.001). Thermal-off for comparison: 0.351 / 0.362 / 0.219. Reason for the move: the wrong-signed latent term had added energy during collapse, inflating the fractional harmonic where the collapse is most violent (near-resonant R0 = 2–3 µm); removing it moved those ratios toward thermal-off, while R0 = 5 µm (weaker collapse, less time above 1056 °C) did not move. The remaining thermal-on/off gap is the genuine thermal-damping effect, strongest at resonance. Thermal-on ratios remain OBSERVED values, not parity targets (see the thermal-model docs in `kwavers-simulation/src/imaging/triplet_pulse.rs`).
  - Accommodation sensitivity run alongside (D12(i)): thermal-on 1.5f0 at α = 0.35 vs 0.04 is 0.727 vs 0.257 (R0 = 2 µm), 0.283 vs 0.299 (R0 = 3 µm), 0.167 vs 0.143 (R0 = 5 µm); at f0 (R0 = 2 µm) 0.145 vs 0.136. The near-resonant ratio is strongly α-dependent; α = 0.04 stays the default.

- **Resolution addendum — dn/dt coupling (Nov 19, 2026):** the "better" form from the proposed fix is now implemented, with no restructuring of the operator split. Assessment of the options:
  - *Reorder the split (mass before temperature)* — rejected as a standalone change: it does not by itself couple the latent term to the applied dn (a pass-forward mechanism is still needed), and it would change the physics (the equilibrium clamp's `n_eq` would be evaluated at the pre-temperature-update `T_gas` instead of the post-update one, and the latent term's `p_vapor` at the post-mass `n_vapor`), moving the validated four-way split and 3P/1P ratios for no coupling benefit.
  - *Pass the applied dn/dt forward via `BubbleState`* — rejected: it needs the reorder above plus a new field on the public `BubbleState` struct (a public API change), and it is stale-by-one-sub-step for any direct caller of `update_temperature` (validation tests call it without a preceding mass update), so it would need freshness bookkeeping.
  - *Share the raw Hertz–Knudsen flux* — contained but insufficient: it prevents future formula drift only; the clamp discrepancy (the actual disagreement) would remain.
  - *Shared applied-Δn helper (implemented):* `update_mass_transfer`'s flux + equilibrium-clamp + zero-floor computation was extracted verbatim into `pub(crate) applied_n_vapor(model, state, dt) -> f64` (post-step `n_vapor`) in `transfer.rs`; `update_mass_transfer` is now a one-line assignment of it plus its existing validity check, and `latent_temperature_rate` (signature now `(model, state, n_moles, c_v, dt)` — the `surface_area` parameter is gone, `dt` added; `pub(crate)` only, no public API change) computes the latent term from the applied Δn: `ṁ = (applied_n_vapor − n_vapor)·M_water/(N_A·dt)`. No reorder, no trait change, no integrator change. The coupling is exact up to one residual: the clamp's `n_eq` depends on `T_gas`, and the latent term sees the pre-temperature-update `T_gas` while the mass update sees the post-update one — an O(ΔT/T) per-sub-step difference, only in clamp-bound sub-steps.
  - Probe measurement (temporary instrumentation, reverted): in the four-way split the equilibrium clamp binds in production — ≥5 binds per run, all observed binds at T ≈ 293 K (near-ambient trimming, raw step overshooting `n_eq` by ~1–3%), none above 400 K, and no zero-floor binds. So the residual `T_gas` difference is negligible in practice, and the coupling is a small correction in production, not a dormant one.
  - Side benefit: the helper unifies the saturation pressure — the latent term previously used Antoine (`p_sat_water_pa`) while the mass update uses Wagner (`thermo_calc.vapor_pressure`), ~0.5% apart at 20 °C (this entry's earlier claim that "the two fluxes agree" was wrong in the strict sense). Both now use Wagner.
  - New test (1): `latent_term_tracks_applied_dn_dt_when_clamp_binds` (`thermodynamics/tests.rs`) — violent condensation at a long step (100 MPa internal pressure, vapour-rich, dt = 1 µs) where the raw flux would drive `n_vapor` far below zero; asserts the mass update lands on `n_eq` (proving the clamp bound) and the latent term's implied mass rate equals the applied dn/dt to 1e-9 relative. Verified to fail against the old raw-flux code: implied ṁ = −3.026e-6 kg/s vs applied −2.972e-10 kg/s (~10⁴×). The two existing `latent_temperature_rate` tests were updated to the new signature only.
  - Gating re-verified airtight: the only production caller of `latent_temperature_rate` is `update_temperature`, itself gated on `use_thermal_effects` at the model level; no other latent-heat term exists in the thermodynamics module (`phase.rs` defines only `L_v`/`p_sat`; `eos.rs` is the Van der Waals pressure). The separate `energy/heat_transfer.rs` latent-heat path (`update_temperature_energy_balance`) has no callers outside its own tests and is not on the Keller–Miksis path.
  - Re-measured post-coupling: full `cargo test -p kwavers-physics --lib`: **1588 passed, 0 failed** (1587 + 1 new); `cargo test -p kwavers-simulation --lib imaging::triplet_pulse`: **11 passed, 0 failed**. Four-way split (3 µm, 800 kPa, 1 MHz, 8 cycles) unchanged: off/off 4.2×, on/off 4.1×, off/on 4.2×, on/on 4.0×. Port thermal-on 3P/1P (0.26 MPa, flat-top, cycles 6–10, α = 0.04): at 1.5f0 **0.248 / 0.302 / 0.143** for R0 = 2 / 3 / 5 µm (was 0.257 / 0.299 / 0.143); at f0 0.136 / 0.034 / 0.001 (unchanged). Accommodation sensitivity at α = 0.35: 1.5f0 0.640 / 0.300 / 0.167 (was 0.727 / 0.283 / 0.167); f0 at R0 = 2 µm 0.135 (was 0.145). Moves are small (≤0.009 at α = 0.04; the α = 0.35 near-resonant move, 0.727 → 0.640, is the clamp binding more at faster equilibration) and all remain inside the runaway guards; thermal-on ratios remain OBSERVED values, not parity targets.
