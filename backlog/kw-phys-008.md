## KW-PHYS-008 — Fragmentation and coalescence population operators (own construction, gas-conserving) [patch] — done (Nov 21, 2026)

priority: feature; needs: none; scope: new module `population/` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/bubble_dynamics/population/`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** bubble-population models can fragment and merge groups through library operators that conserve gas exactly, expose their kernels as parameters, and carry their status — own construction, not literature law — in their documentation.
- **Driver:** Builds 5–7 of the triplet-pulse formation work built these operators ad hoc in the Python prototype (binned populations, lognormal daughter transfer, neighbour-probability merging). No daughter-distribution or merging operator existed in the crate (only breakup *detection* via shape instability, and a scalar equal-size clearance estimate in `analytical/cavitation/swept_frequency/clearance.rs`). Ryan's Nov 21, 2026 direction — all mechanisms implemented as reusable library physics for the multi-physics suite — covers own-construction mechanisms too, provided they are marked as such.
- **Acceptance:** both kernels and the merge operator conserve total gas to rounding in tests; lognormal daughters are truncated to ≤ the parent radius; the Chomas et al. (2001) fragmentation indicator (max/min diameter ratio > 10) is the gate helper; the one fitted parameter in the area (`P_FRAG_FITTED_LI_2021 = 0.02`, Stage 7 calibration to Li et al. 2021) is named and documented as fitted.

### Resolution (Nov 21, 2026)

- **Implementation.** `population/mod.rs`: `BubbleGroup { radius, count, gas_per_bubble }` (real-valued ensemble counts) and `PopulationMedium` tying radius ↔ gas through the KW-PHYS-005 equilibrium map. `population/fragmentation.rs`: the `FragmentationKernel` trait (static dispatch) with `EqualSplitKernel` (k equal daughters, radius from the equilibrium map at n/k) and `LognormalKernel` (lognormal daughters, median ratio × parent, truncated to ≤ parent, discretised onto log-spaced bins; bin counts from gas ÷ equilibrium gas at bin radius, so conservation is exact by construction); `collapse_ratio` and the Chomas constant. `population/coalescence.rs`: `neighbour_merge_probability(r_max, count, focal_volume)` = `1 − exp(−(4π/3)(2R_max)³N/V)`; `merge_pair`; `coalesce_group` (fraction pairs up, gas pools to 2n, radius from the map).
- **Physical-use warnings carried in the docs.** (1) Per-cycle re-application of coalescence with re-randomised pairing is unphysical for bubbles fixed in tissue — it was the only variant producing a >10 µm tail in the formation model, an artefact (Build 7); the operator is one-shot or tracked-pairing. (2) Above MI ≈ 1, burst-phase coalescence is excluded by the Duryea et al. (2015) cliff (KW-PHYS-007 classifier). (3) Fragmentation alone can never grow a large-radius tail, because daughters are truncated to ≤ the parent — a structural property of the kernel, stated so no future model mistakes its absence for a numerical failure. (4) **Fragmentation is single-sequence only (Build 11, Nov 21, 2026):** iterated per sequence over the 200× Iwasaki exposure it has no fixed point (counts ×885 by sequence 10, reservoir exhausted ~sequence 25–30, sub-µm dust). Build 11 also closed the removal inversion negative — holding the multi-sequence fixed point at the measured ~3 µm residuals would require ≈24× Epstein–Plesset dissolution (band 12–29×), and all candidate literature channels reach ~13% of that — so **no per-sequence removal term is implemented**, deliberately.
- **Tests.** Gas conservation to <1e-9 relative for both kernels and for `coalesce_group`; count arithmetic (equal-split ×8 at full fraction; coalescence 1000 → 750 at f = 0.5); truncation; neighbour probability at the formation point (N = 1054 in 6.28 mm³, R_max = 51.2 µm → p ≈ 0.53) and its sparse/dense limits; merge-pair radius within 10% of the equal-volume 2^(1/3) expectation.
