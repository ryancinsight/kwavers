## KW-GPU-FFT-PLAN-CAPACITY — A GPU PSTD plan whose FFT workspace or dispatch exceeds a device limit is refused as `InvalidInput`, after field buffers exist [patch] — blocked

priority: correctness; needs: none; basis: 9b2b75c590efb6503f92d01aee2fae79ef73690e; scope: `crates/kwavers-gpu/src/pstd_gpu/pipeline/`, `crates/kwavers-simulation/src/runner.rs`

- **Origin.** The remainder of `KW-GPU-PLAN-CAPACITY`, whose acceptance asks for the refusal "before the run allocates field buffers". #1059 (open at filing) adds a check of the buffers the provider sizes itself; its first verdict (comment 6814685775, finding 1) shows that the FFT plan Hephaestus prepares is outside that check.
- **Read at the basis.** `build_state` creates the seven field buffers from line 108 of `crates/kwavers-gpu/src/pstd_gpu/pipeline/construction/solver.rs` and the two k-space buffers at lines 128 and 131, and only then prepares the FFT plans (lines 146 and 149); `with_auto_device` wraps the failure as `InvalidInput` (`pipeline/auto_device.rs` line 57). At the locked Hephaestus revision (72ffa0577952411a27182782637248a2c8a02ead), `hephaestus-wgpu/src/application/fft/plan.rs` gives a non-power-of-two axis of length n a workspace of m values per line, m the power of two at or above 2n - 1 (line 73). It refuses a volume, workspace or twiddle buffer over `max_buffer_size` (line 228) and a workspace of more than 256 values (`WORKGROUP_SIZE`, `kernel.rs` line 10) times `max_compute_workgroups_per_dimension` (line 236), each as `HephaestusError::InvalidConfiguration`, whose one field is a message.
- **Run by the judge of #1059** at d186187366fe733d3b7f17e15ba744b8d787f26f: `SimulationRunner::run` on 129x256x264 returned `InvalidInput` ending "FFT workspace requires 138412032 bytes, exceeding device max_buffer_size=134217728"; `GpuPstdSolver::new` on 17x16x16 at a `max_buffer_size` of 61440 returned a message ending "FFT workspace requires 65536 bytes, exceeding device max_buffer_size=61440". **Run by the integrator** on an export of that head: `SimulationRunner::run` on 200x200x200 returned `InvalidInput` ending "FFT dispatch requires 80000 x workgroups, exceeding device max_compute_workgroups_per_dimension=65535", with and without a sensor point. No run panicked. Derived: the #1059 check admits all three: `pml_xyz` needs 104620032, 52224 and 96000000 bytes and `shifts_all` 10384, 784 and 9600, each under the `max_buffer_size` of its run (134217728 bytes, and 61440 for 17x16x16); the binding limit of the judge's device is not reported.
- **Blocker:** Hephaestus has no query for what a plan needs and no typed capacity refusal, and a copy of its strategy selection in this crate would drift from it. `HEPH-FFT-PLAN-REQUIREMENTS` on the Hephaestus board holds both. **Re-open trigger:** that item lands on Hephaestus `master` and `Cargo.lock` here pins a commit that has it.
- **Acceptance:** each of the three requests returns `KwaversError::ResourceLimitExceeded` naming what exceeds, the size it needs and the limit, before the provider creates a buffer: 17x16x16 through `GpuPstdSolver::new` (as its message) on a device acquired with that limit, the other two through `with_auto_device` and `SimulationRunner::run`, which take no limit; device tests assert the variant and the whole message; a plan Hephaestus prepares is still admitted; a check moved after the first buffer fails a test.
- **Next step:** after the trigger, ask Hephaestus for the plan's requirements in the provider's capacity check, before `build_state`.
