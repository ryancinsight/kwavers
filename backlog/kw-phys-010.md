## KW-PHYS-010 — Kreider et al. (2011) reduced-form gain law (Eller–Flynn convolution) missing from the library [patch] — done (Nov 21, 2026)

priority: feature; needs: none; scope: new module `kreider_convolution.rs` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/mechanics/cavitation/core/`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** the library carries the Kreider et al. (2011, *JASA* 130, 3511–3530) reduced form as a reusable component: Eq. (14) Eller–Flynn convolution for the non-condensable gas content with history carried in transformed time s = ∫R⁴dt across all cycles, Eq. (12) thermal pressure as a first-law energy balance, Eq. (15) Hertz–Knudsen vapour kinetics with the Eq. (16) trapping cap, and the SCL wall-temperature fallback — with its validation status and the three Build 12 flags documented in the module docs.
- **Driver:** Build 12 of the triplet-pulse formation work (Nov 21, 2026) implemented and benchmarked this law in the prototype as a candidate replacement for the Bader Eq. (7) trigger influx (KW-PHYS-005). It was **not adopted** there (differential-gate FAIL, below), and is handed to the library per the standing direction that mechanisms are implemented as modular components with validation status and regime documented whether or not the formation model adopts them.
- **Acceptance:** the convolution's fixed-R reduction reproduces the Epstein–Plesset transient uptake exactly (the derivation that verifies the Eq. (14) prefactor); the no-renewal limit r(25) = √(2/25) is reproduced; p_sat anchors hold through the crate's existing Wagner model; the integrator is stable and correctly signed on a quiescent history.

### Resolution (Nov 21, 2026)

- **Implementation.** `EllerFlynnHistory` (segment list in s, product integration with analytic per-segment kernel weights, s-weighted compression, gross in/out split for diagnostics); `KreiderGainModel` (state n_g, n_v, θ̄ + history; `integrate_cycles` consumes caller-supplied Keller–Miksis radius histories block by block, ≤ 5 cycles per block, with the Kreider Eq. (28) lagged-update scheme — F evaluated at cycle-start n_g, cycle-end n_g solved exactly from the full history — and the truncation guard: steps with wall Mach > 1 or R below the hard core h = R_eq/8.76 advance s with F = 0 and are counted); `wall_temperature_scl` (SCL fallback; the Eq. (22) PZ convolution is NOT implemented); `vapour_rate_hertz_knudsen` (Eq. (15) + Eq. (16) cap); `latent_heat_molar` (Watson form, → 0 at T_c, KW-PHYS-003 clamp). `p_sat(T_w)` reuses the crate's existing Wagner model (`bubble_dynamics/thermodynamics::ThermodynamicsCalculator`) rather than duplicating coefficients. Gas contents are moles inside the module (Kreider's convention); callers convert explicitly. Constants carry their provenance: α = 0.4 as published; **A_m = 0.8 and f_m = 0.998 are Kreider's fitted constants** (lithotripsy rebounds, §IV.A), named `KREIDER_A_M_FITTED` / `KREIDER_F_M_FITTED`, never re-tuned.
- **The three flags (in the module docs, verbatim in substance).** (1) **Veto-gate PASS**: at the Bader et al. (2021) agarose anchor the prototype predicts residual 2.477 µm vs measured 2.9 ± 0.1 µm (−14.6 %, ±20 % gate) — with Eq. (7) closer at the same anchor (2.655 µm, −8.4 %); the claim is mechanistic defensibility, not a benchmark win. (2) **Differential-gate FAIL in the formation context**: the law cuts the trigger influx to 0.262× (Kuji, 25 cycles) / 0.120× (Iwasaki, 125 cycles) of Eq. (7) — a pure cycle-number effect landing on the derived, unpublished no-renewal bound √(2/N) — breaking the Kuji match (needs ≥ 0.81) while fixing Iwasaki; **not adopted as the formation trigger law**. (3) **Burst-regime caveat**: no periodic steady state at burst conditions; per-cycle influx 22.6–60.3× above Fyrillas–Szeri/Louisnard–Gomez (KW-PHYS-004) there — do not use as a burst law.
- **Equation verification status (documented in the module).** Eq. (14) prefactor verified by derivation (fixed-R reduction = Epstein–Plesset transient, prototype rel. err 1.5×10⁻¹⁵); Eq. (12)'s exact printed bracket UNVERIFIED (garbled in the source text layer — implemented in the equivalent first-law form); Eq. (22) PZ not implemented (SCL fallback, as the lit specification permits); H taken as C0/p0 with temperature dependence not implemented (Kreider Table I unavailable — flagged in the prototype).
- **Tests.** p_sat anchors (6.28 kPa @ 37 °C, 101.3 kPa @ 100 °C, P_c at T_c); L_v(T_c) = 0; convolution → Epstein–Plesset transient at fixed R (< 1 %) and r(25) = √(2/25) in the same run; vapour-rate signs and the T_c shutoff; quiescent-history integrator (small signed outflow at C\* = 1, no choke events). The veto/differential numbers are prototype results documented in the module docs, not re-asserted in Rust — a full trigger integration needs Keller–Miksis histories at 20 MPa, the KW-PHYS-009 item 5 gap.
