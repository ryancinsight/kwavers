## KW-PHYS-014 — Cycle-averaged rectified diffusion (Fyrillas–Szeri / Louisnard–Gomez) missing from the library [patch] — done (Nov 21, 2026)

> Renumbered from KW-PHYS-004 on 2026-11-22: that ID was independently used on main for an unrelated item (see reconcile/conflicts/CONFLICT_kw-phys-004_005.md in the kwavers-work records).

priority: feature/correctness; needs: none; scope: new module `louisnard_gomez.rs` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/mechanics/cavitation/core/`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** the library carries the cycle-averaged rectified-diffusion law that applies at inertial (burst) pressures, where the Eller–Flynn steady rate of KW-PHYS-001 is invalid: Louisnard & Gomez (2003, *Phys. Rev. E* 67, 036610) Eq. (16), driven by a Keller–Miksis radius history, with its validated regime stated in code and a checked variant that refuses extrapolation outside it.
- **Driver:** Build 6 of the triplet-pulse formation work (Nov 20, 2026) established that the Bader Eq. (7) influx does not apply at burst pressures in any form (it is a single-pulse formula for short shocked pulses) and that the Fyrillas & Szeri (1994) cycle-averaged theory, in the Louisnard & Gomez formulation, is the correct replacement — per-cycle influx 542× (Kuji) / 324× (Iwasaki) smaller than Eq. (7) at R₀ = 5 µm. Fyrillas & Szeri was previously a reference-list mention only; Louisnard & Gomez had no presence in the crate.
- **Acceptance:** the law reproduces the paper's Fig. 4 growth rates at 26.5 kHz in saturated water within the figure-read band (factor ~2; the prototype's validation read them within ~10%); the cycle-average functional is exact on analytic histories; the regime flags mark the validated box (20 kHz < f < 100 kHz, 1 bar < p < 5 bar, R₀ < 15 µm, near-saturated).

### Resolution (Nov 21, 2026)

- **Implementation.** `louisnard_gomez_growth_rate(radius, time, equilibrium_radius, saturation_ratio, params)` evaluates Eq. (16), `dR0/dt = [D·R_G·T·C0 / (p0·R0·(1 + 2α_S/3))] · (C∞* − ⟨pg/p0⟩₄) / I`, with `⟨pg/p0⟩₄ = ⟨(pg/p0)R⁴⟩/⟨R⁴⟩` (Eq. 8), the van der Waals isothermal gas pressure (Eq. 2, `pg = pg0(R0³−h³)/(R³−h³)`, `h = R0/8.76`), and the dissipation integral `I` (Eq. 7) evaluated by the prototype's substitution `u = 3s/Rmax*³` on a log grid with the analytic tail. Also: `cycle_average_terms` (exposes ⟨pg/p0⟩₄, I, Rmax*), `louisnard_gomez_per_cycle_influx` (molecules/cycle through the equilibrium gas map), `louisnard_gomez_regime` (flags struct, following the D_MAX flag pattern), and `louisnard_gomez_growth_rate_checked` (refuses outside the validated regime with `PhysicsError::InvalidParameter`). Plain `f64` + unit comments, matching `growth.rs` style.
- **Benchmark.** The Fig. 4 validation integrates Keller–Miksis histories at the paper's conditions (σ = 0.073, μ = 0.001, κ = 1, T = 293 K, D = 2.0e-9, C0 = 0.83 mol/m³, C* = 1) against the paper's anchors — (R₀ µm, p bar) → dR0/dt µm/s: (3, 2.0) ~200; (5, 1.3) ~30; (5, 2.0) ~100; (5, 5.0) ~300; (10, 2.0) ~30; (20, 2.0) ~10 — within a factor-of-2 band plus ordering. The (5 µm, 2 bar) anchor is an always-on test; a four-anchor sweep ((5, 2.0), (10, 2.0), (20, 2.0), (5, 1.3)) is an `#[ignore]`d benchmark and passes in release. The two strongest anchors are excluded from the Rust sweep because their histories defeat the explicit test driver: (3 µm, 2 bar) stalls it in the stiff rebound, and at (5 µm, 5 bar) the driver's tolerance-converged history departs qualitatively from LSODA's (it settles into a non-collapsing mode). Both are validated by the prototype's LSODA integration (222.9 and 275.3 µm/s vs the paper's ~200 and ~300 µm/s) — the same implicit-integrator gap as item 5 below. Exact tests pin the functional: constant history gives ⟨pg/p0⟩₄ = 1 + α_S and I = 1 exactly; the u-substituted I matches direct s-integration within 5%.
- **History source — a library gap found.** The test integrates a test-local transcription of the prototype's validated Keller–Miksis RHS (formation/b6a_law.py `km_rhs`), because the library's own KM cannot produce histories in this regime: `calculate_acceleration` errors above wall Mach 0.95 and the adaptive integrator's stability monitor rejects |Ṙ| > 0.1·c; at (3 µm, 2 bar) the bubble expands to ~102 µm and collapses through both bounds, deadlocking the integrator (`ConvergenceFailure`). Filed as KW-PHYS-009 item 5.
- **Not implemented, deliberately.** The Smith & Wang history-integral law (JFM 939, A28) is catastrophic for inertial bubbles (Build 6 V2) and is not ported. The paper's Fig. 6 growth-time comparison is PARTIAL in the prototype (its App. A fit constants do not reproduce the paper's own integral); the integral here is evaluated directly, as validated against Fig. 7's I = 0.0708.
