## KW-GPU-ADAPTER-FIELD-STATES — GPU PSTD adapter accessor docs name no value before a batch, and the Rustdoc of `pressure_field` is contradicted after a peak run [patch] — todo

priority: verification; needs: none; basis: fa8c4a892787ec8907ddb504ac3a7fb62bf3874c; scope: `crates/kwavers-simulation/src/solver_adapters/gpu_pstd.rs`, `docs/adr/037-gpu-pstd-output-contract.md`

- **Origin.** Found while reading the adapter for #1053; the first verdict there (comment 6810004764, F8) pointed to the peak run, and the first verdict on #1055 (comment 6812387488, F1) to the accessor's Rustdoc and to ADR 037.
- **Read at the basis.** `GpuPstdSimulationAdapter::new` fills its pressure and three velocity arrays with zeros (`gpu_pstd.rs` lines 123 to 126). `run_peak_pressure` (line 153) calls `store_peak_pressure` and not `store_final_fields`, and its doc says it does not download final pressure or velocity frames (lines 144 and 145); so after it the four arrays keep what the last `run` stored, or the zeros. The Rustdoc of `pressure_field` (line 377) says it returns "the final GPU-read pressure field from the most recent batch". That names no value before a batch, and it is contradicted by the reading above when the most recent batch is a peak run. `velocity_fields` (line 382) has no Rustdoc; the module doc says "after `run()`" of both. `statistics` (line 390) folds the three stored velocity arrays into `max_velocity` in every state.
- **Tests at the basis.** `git grep -n -E "[.]pressure_field\(\)|velocity_fields\(\)" -- crates/kwavers-simulation/src/solver_adapters/gpu_pstd` finds four reads on a new adapter, each of the shape only (`tests.rs` lines 25, 70, 87 and 104), two reads after `store_final_fields` (lines 141 and 146) and one after `run` in a test ignored by default (line 209).
- **Acceptance:** the Rustdoc of the accessors and ADR 037 state what the four arrays and `max_velocity` hold before a batch and after `run_peak_pressure`, no accessor doc is contradicted in either state, and a test without a device asserts each stated value.
- **Next step:** ADR 037 lists "Retain zero arrays with documentation" among its rejected alternatives (line 75) and says the adapter "exposes actual provider values rather than zero arrays" (lines 10 and 11). Documenting the present zeros therefore needs a revision of that record. Decide between that revision and a change under which the two states are not readable as fields; the second changes what a caller of the accessors gets.
