## KW-PHYS-007 — Bjerknes forces had no validity-regime guards; module header documented a formula matching neither implementation [patch] — done (Nov 21, 2026)

priority: correctness; needs: none; scope: `kwavers-physics` `acoustics/bubble_dynamics/bjerknes_forces/` (new `regime.rs`; header correction in `mod.rs`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** the secondary Bjerknes force can no longer be evaluated silently in the regime where its linear derivation has no meaning, the measured aggregation window (Duryea et al. 2015) is available as a classifier, and the module header documents the formulas actually implemented.
- **Driver:** Build 8 of the triplet-pulse formation work (Nov 20, 2026) audited Bjerknes transport as a candidate tail-growth mechanism and found the linear theory violated at the formation conditions on every axis: displacement ratio ξ/R₀ = 6–209× (linear theory needs ≪ 1), neighbour radiated pressure 26–60% of the drive (weak coupling needs ≪), and the naive uncoupled estimate predicts a first merge in 0.05 µs against observed cloud persistence ≥ 600 µs. The kwavers implementation had no guard against exactly this misuse. Separately, the audit found the `mod.rs` header's secondary-force formula `(6πρc₀/f)·V₁V₂·cos(φ)/d²` matches neither `secondary.rs` (time-averaged harmonic form) nor `interactions.rs` (instantaneous Crum form), and misspelled Bjerknes.
- **Acceptance:** a checked variant refuses pairs at ξ/R₀ ≥ 1 with `PhysicsError::InvalidParameter`; the Duryea classifier bands MI correctly (window 0.2–1.0; formation points 6.0/14.7 → AboveCliff); the header states the implemented primary (`−(4π/3)R³∇p`) and secondary (`(ρ₀ω²/8π)·ΔV₁ΔV₂·cos(φ)/d²`) forms and points at the Crum form's location.

### Resolution (Nov 21, 2026)

- **Implementation.** New `bjerknes_forces/regime.rs`: `bjerknes_regime(...)` → `BjerknesRegime { displacement_ratio_1, displacement_ratio_2 }` with `linear_regime()` (both < 1) and `small_amplitude()` (both < 0.1); `displacement_amplitude(ΔV, R)` = |ΔV|/(4πR²); `radiated_pressure_amplitude(ΔV, f, d, ρ)` = ρω²|ΔV|/(4πd) for the weak-coupling check; `duryea_aggregation_regime(mi)` → `AggregationRegime { BelowWindow, AggregationWindow, AboveCliff }` with the measured bounds as constants; `secondary_bjerknes_force_checked(...)` wrapping the calculator with the ξ/R₀ refusal. Header of `mod.rs` rewritten to the implemented formulas with the regime section pointing at the guards. Docs record why no strong-field closed form is offered: the strong-field literature (Mettin 1997; Pelekasis 2004; Lanoy 2015; Pandey 2019; Doinikov 2001) is numerical/qualitative — sign reversal, bound pairs — with no closed-form law near the formation conditions.
- **Not done here.** The duplicate implementations themselves (secondary in two forms, primary in three places) are consolidation work, filed as KW-PHYS-009 items 1–2 — this entry adds the guards and corrects the documentation without moving existing call sites.
