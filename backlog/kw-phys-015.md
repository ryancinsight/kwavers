## KW-PHYS-015 — Bader-family expansion-phase influx, D_MAX fit, and residual-radius maps missing from the library [patch] — done (Nov 21, 2026)

> Renumbered from KW-PHYS-005 on 2026-11-22: that ID was independently used on main for an unrelated item (see reconcile/conflicts/CONFLICT_kw-phys-004_005.md in the kwavers-work records).

priority: feature; needs: none; scope: new module `bader_influx.rs` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/mechanics/cavitation/core/`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** the library carries the histotripsy bubble-formation gas budget as reusable physics: the Bader & Bollen (2018) nonlinear expansion-phase diffusive influx with its Henry-law surface-concentration correction, the compression-phase outflux integral, the Bader & Holland (2016) D_MAX correlation with fit-range flags, and the equilibrium/residual gas↔radius maps — each with its validated regime enforced or flagged.
- **Driver:** Build 4 of the triplet-pulse formation work (Nov 20, 2026) showed the expansion-phase influx closes the formation gas budget by ~10⁸× over the pre-Build-4 transport (Kuji cloud receives 61× the gas it needs at C = 0.1, 547× at C = 0.9) and is the validated influx law for shock-scattering histotripsy; none of it existed in the crate. The R_nuc question (whether the maps' radius is the pre-exposure nucleus) was resolved from the papers' text the same day (RNUC_RESOLUTION.md): it is the nucleus in all three papers.
- **Acceptance:** Benchmark B anchors reproduced (Bader & Bollen 2018 residual diameters: 1 cycle at 18.3 MPa, 90% saturation → 0.80 µm paper vs prototype 0.70 µm; 3 cycles at 14.5 MPa → 0.95 vs 0.92); the 10 nm nucleus equilibrium gas content ≈ 1.11×10⁴ molecules at 310 K; the implicit and as-printed residual maps both provided, implicit primary.

### Resolution (Nov 21, 2026)

- **Implementation.** `bader_expansion_influx(r_max, t_m, diffusivity, far_field_concentration)` — `dn = 8·C·√(π·D·t_M)·R_M²` in molecules (concentrations in molecules/m³); `bader_signed_expansion_influx` subtracts the Henry surface term `C_s = C_0·P_g(R_M)/P_0` (Bader et al. 2019a Eqs. (4)–(6) as generalised in Build 6); `compression_phase_outflux` integrates `4πR²D(C_inf − C_s(R))/δ(t)` over a caller-supplied history with `δ(t) = √(πDt)` and `P_g(R) = P_gM(R_M/R)^{3γ}`, where `R_min` is the pressure-balance radius; `bader_holland_dmax(p−, f)` returns the diameter with `BaderHollandFlags` (fit range p− 15–25 MPa, f 0.75–1.0 MHz, 5–20 cycles; continual-growth threshold 14.7 MPa); `equilibrium_gas_content` / `equilibrium_radius_for_pressure` (bisection on `p·R³ + 2σR² = 3k_BT·n/(4π)`) / `residual_radius_equilibrium` (implicit map, primary) / `residual_radius_as_printed` (sensitivity). `bader_expansion_influx_checked` refuses outside the validated influx regime (p− 14.5–40 MPa, ≤ 5 cycles).
- **Regime honesty.** The influx law is validated for short shocked pulses (Bader & Bollen 2018: 14.5–18.3 MPa, 3–5 cycles, 90% saturation; Bader 2021: 25–40 MPa, 2 cycles). Build 6 established it does **not** extend to burst pressures cycle-by-cycle; the module docs say so, and the checked variant enforces the cycle bound. D_MAX is a fit (r² = 0.999, RMSE 0.62 µm over its range) — outside its range the function still returns the fit value but the flags say it is extrapolated, because flagging (not refusing) is what a correlation is for.
- **Tests.** Nucleus gas content within 2% of 1.11×10⁴; n → R → n round-trips at 0.5/3/10 µm; as-printed vs implicit diameters at n = 10⁹ (≈0.90 µm vs ≈3.5 µm); D_MAX flags (in-range point clean; the Iwasaki burst point 5.39 MPa / 1.25 MHz sets three flags); Benchmark B diameters within a ±20% band; checked-variant refusals; signed influx reduces to the unsigned law at C_s = 0 and reverses for C_s > C_inf.
