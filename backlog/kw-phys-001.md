## KW-PHYS-001 — Rectified-diffusion rate is not the Eller–Flynn rate (missing oscillation-amplitude factor) [patch] — done (Nov 19, 2026)

priority: correctness; needs: none; scope: `rectified_diffusion_rate` in `kwavers-physics` (`acoustics/mechanics/cavitation/core/growth.rs`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** `rectified_diffusion_rate` returns the genuine Eller–Flynn rate: at Eller's (1969) conditions it gives growth of the order of the measured ~13 µm/min, not 515–527 µm/min — so the function is the theory it claims to implement, and no caller can mistake its output for a validated growth rate.
- **Driver:** Stage 7 of the triplet-pulse prototype (Nov 17, 2026) found the kwavers formula returns 515–527 µm/min at Eller's 1969 conditions against ~13 µm/min measured — a 40× overprediction — while the genuine Eller–Flynn theory UNDERpredicts the same measurement by >20× (Smith & Wang 2022, J. Fluid Mech. 939, A28 §1). The kwavers formula is the Eller–Flynn form with the oscillation amplitude set to unity; the amplitude factor (ε-dependent) is missing. The Stage 6 audit's retired numbers (986/1904 µm) and its shell/depletion bounds are rate-independent and unaffected. The regime guard (`rectified_diffusion_rate_checked`, refuses P_ac/P0 > 3) is already in place; this entry is the rate correction itself.
- **Acceptance:** rate at Eller's conditions within a factor of ~2 of the measured ~13 µm/min (or of the genuine Eller–Flynn value with the amplitude factor restored); the existing `growth` tests still pass, including the checked-variant guard tests; the amplitude dependence is documented on the function, with Smith & Wang (2022) noted as the more accurate in-regime alternative (two-ODE model: radius + gas mass, fading-memory history integral).

### Resolution (Nov 19, 2026)

- **Root cause.** The old formula `4πR·D·C·(P_ac/P_0)·√Pe` was not the Eller–Flynn rate at all: it was linear in the pressure ratio with no oscillation-amplitude dependence, carried a spurious `√Pe` factor (the genuine `√Pe`-like term is only the *transient* `R_0/√(πDt)`, which tends to 1 at long times and is not part of the steady rate), and had no Laplace dissolution term. It was ~1000× the genuine rectified flux (~790× the growth term, ~1040× the net flux at 30 µm).
- **Fix.** Replaced with the genuine generalised Eller–Flynn steady (long-time) rate of Crum & Hansen (1982, JASA 72, 1586–1592) as restated by Crum & Mao (1996, p. 2902) and Zhang (2012, PhD thesis, §3.2, eqs. (3.6)–(3.19)): net molar flux `4πR_0·D·C_0·⟨R/R_0⟩·(1 − ⟨(R/R_0)⁴(P_in/P_0)⟩/⟨(R/R_0)⁴⟩)`, second order in the fractional oscillation amplitude `ε = |α|(P_ac/P_0)`, split into a rectified growth term and a Laplace dissolution term, so the returned flux is *signed* (growth above threshold, dissolution below). Thermal function Φ of Devin (1959, eq. 50) / Prosperetti et al. (1988, eq. 41), with the effective polytropic exponent `κ = Re Φ/3` (Prosperetti 1977, JASA 61, 17) used in every equation; thermal damping per Crum & Mao eq. (3.10), `δ_th = Im Φ/Re Φ`. Signature unchanged; `rectified_diffusion_rate_checked` guard unchanged. New public API: `rectified_diffusion_terms(...)` returning a `RectifiedDiffusionTerms` struct (net/growth/dissolution flux, ε, κ, δ_tot), and `oscillation_amplitude_fraction(...)` so callers can check `ε ≪ 1`. The old docstring's Church (1988) citation (wrong title/pages) was corrected.
- **Two corrections found while implementing.** (a) The K-factor denominator's surface term is `2σ/(R_0P_0)` (thesis eq. (3.15)), not `σ/(R_0P_0)`. (b) The thesis uses the thermal κ everywhere in the dynamics (ω₀², β², damping, K, cycle averages), not the adiabatic γ — an adiabatic cross-check understates ε and the growth term by ~30% (threshold 55 µm / 32 kPa with γ vs 47 µm / 27 kPa with κ). The Rust implementation and an independent Python implementation agree to 6+ significant figures on every intermediate (ε, K, κ, δ_tot, β², averages, brackets, fluxes) at four check points.
- **Measured numbers — Eller (1969) benchmark** (26.6 kHz, 20 kPa, D = 2e-9 m²/s, C = 0.26 mol/m³; dR/dt via isothermal ideal-gas `dn/dR`):

  | R₀ (µm) | net (µm/min) | growth term only (µm/min) | old formula |
  |--------:|-------------:|--------------------------:|------------:|
  | 20 | −1.65 | +0.94 | 516 |
  | 30 | −0.50 | +0.66 | 524 |
  | 40 | −0.13 | +0.53 | 528 |
  | 50 | +0.05 | +0.47 | 530 |

  Measured: ~13 µm/min, flat 30–50 µm. Threshold radius ≈ 47 µm; threshold pressure at 30 µm ≈ 27 kPa. ε ≈ 0.065 at all four radii. Growth flux scales ∝ P_ac² (ratio 3.98 for 5→10 kPa) and exactly ∝ C.
- **Acceptance, honestly stated.** The growth term (0.47–0.94 µm/min) is within 2× of Eller's own theoretical value (~0.6 µm/min) — the "genuine Eller–Flynn value" branch of the acceptance criterion. It is NOT within 2× of the *measured* ~13 µm/min, and no genuine-theory implementation can be: the literature establishes the >20× theory-vs-measurement gap (Smith & Wang 2022 §1; Gould 1974, JASA 56, 1740–1746; Church 1988, JASA 83, 2210–2217). No factor was fitted to force agreement with Eller; the numbers above are the theory as published. The remaining gap is missing physics (see "Not fixed"), not a formula error.
- **Tests.** Physics suite 1591 passed / 0 failed (baseline 1588/0; +3 new tests). Growth module 10/10: new `test_eller_1969_benchmark`, `test_eller_threshold_pressure`, `test_thermal_phi_limits` (Φ adiabatic/isothermal limits, δ_th vs Devin eqs. (2.50)/(2.52)); `test_rectified_diffusion_amplitude_scaling` replaces the old linearity test (which encoded the bug: linear in P_ac, √Pe) with P_ac² growth scaling + exact C-linearity; `test_rectified_diffusion_zero_acoustic_pressure` updated (net flux is now negative — Laplace dissolution — growth term exactly 0.0); `test_rectified_diffusion_zero_diffusivity` now asserts exactly 0.0 (no Pe division, so the old NaN allowance is gone); `test_rectified_diffusion_positive_flux` now uses an above-threshold point (30 µm / 35 kPa / 26.6 kHz, +0.75 µm/min) and documents that the old 5 µm / 120 kPa / 1 MHz point is below threshold (net −11.6 µm/min, growth term +25 µm/min). All three `rectified_diffusion_rate_checked` guard tests unchanged and passing. `cargo fmt --check` clean, no warnings.
- **Not fixed, and why.**
  - *Microstreaming (the >20× gap to Eller's measurement).* Church (1988, JASA 84, 1758–1764) is the published microstreaming correction, but its full text was not accessible for this fix, so no streaming term was implemented. Adding it requires reading Church (1988, JASA 84) and benchmarking against Eller (1969); it must not be fitted to the 13 µm/min.
  - *Early-time growth.* This is the long-time (quasi-steady) rate; the early-time rate is asymptotically larger (Smith & Wang 2022, JFM 939, A28). Their two-ODE model (JFM 951, A12) remains the more accurate in-regime alternative; neither is ported here.
  - *Supersaturation.* The single `concentration` argument is used as both C₀ and C_i (saturated liquid, C_i/C₀ = 1). Supersaturated or undersaturated liquids need the split.
