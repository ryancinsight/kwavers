## KW-PHYS-013 — Foldy–Lax self-consistent coupled bubble driving (multiple scattering) [patch] — done (Nov 22, 2026)

priority: feature; needs: none; scope: `kwavers-physics` bubble dynamics (`acoustics/bubble_dynamics/coupled_driving.rs`); milestone: none

- **Outcome:** bubble-cloud driving is self-consistent — each bubble's Keller–Miksis solve is driven by the incident pressure history plus the sum of its neighbours' retarded radiated monopole fields — as a library component with its validated regime documented, replacing the independent-driving approximation every earlier cloud path used.
- **Driver:** D14 flagged the dense-cloud correction (shadowing, multiple scattering, coupled oscillation) as unmeasured; Build 15 WS-B (`B15_WSB.md`) measured it with a Foldy–Lax Python reference (`kwavers-work/b15/km_coupled.py`): the correction is real but small on the headline statistic and partly wrong-signed, and it is library knowledge worth keeping in code under Ryan's 2026-11-21 library rule.
- **Acceptance:** Foldy–Lax coupling `p_i(t) = p_inc(t) + Σ_{j≠i} (ρ/4πd_ij)·V̈_j(t − d_ij/c)` reusing the existing Keller–Miksis model (no duplicated KM equation); damped Picard iteration θ = 0.5 (plain Picard oscillates on close pairs; θ changes only the path — the coupling field is linear in the V̈ history, so the fixed point is θ-independent); validation and regime documented in code; tests for the dilute limit, two-bubble symmetry, and Picard convergence.

### Resolution (Nov 22, 2026)

- **Implementation.** `bubble_dynamics/coupled_driving.rs`, sibling of `cloud_attenuation.rs` (a cloud-level coherent field solve; the `population/` module holds ensemble bookkeeping operators on group counts, which this is not): `pair_geometry` (weights `ρ/(4πd)`, delays `d/c`), `coupling_field` (retarded monopole sum on the grid), `solve_coupled_cloud` (generic over `AdaptiveBubbleModel`; per-pass total drive precomputed on the grid, derivative by centred differences, RK4 in the reference's stage arrangement), `segment_band_power` (1.5f₀ steady-segment diagnostic), `coupled_regime_reasons` (regime guard), `PICARD_DAMPING_THETA = 0.5`. Re-exported from `bubble_dynamics/mod.rs`.
- **Validation (WS-B, carried in the module docs).** Dilute limit (density ×0.008): coupled reproduces independent driving, 1.5f₀ band power −0.11 dB; KM RHS cross-check vs pykwavers within 2.7 % at ≤ 0.27 MPa and 3.5 % at 0.73 MPa. Rust tests reproduce the dilute-limit recovery (band power within 0.2 dB), two-identical-bubble symmetry, and damped-Picard convergence to a fixed point on a close pair.
- **Measured effect (WS-B).** Δ cavitation reduction at fc 3.0 MHz: +0.19 / +0.83 / +2.44 / −1.94 dB at 0.123 / 0.268 / 0.729 / 1.273 MPa — +0.83 dB of the 10.5 dB gap at 0.26 MPa (≈ 8 %), sign-reversing at the top PNP. No shadowing at imaging conditions: the cloud-centre drive is ENHANCED +0.9 to +2.6 dB RMS.
- **Regime warning carried in the docs.** Validated at imaging conditions only (2.45 MHz, 0.12–1.27 MPa, R0 ≈ 2–2.8 µm, densities to ≈ 128 bubbles/mm³); NOT validated at trigger pressures. The KW-PHYS-006 extinction α is an incoherent energy quantity and must NOT be imported as a local drive reduction — the coherent sum can have the opposite sign (centre measurement implies an effective α of −80 to −246 Np/m).
